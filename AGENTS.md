# Working in stack

Use UK English in responses and original prose. Prefer spellings such as "organise", "behaviour" and "colour". Preserve exact quotations, proper names, code identifiers, commands and externally defined strings.

Keep reusable skills in `skills/<name>/SKILL.md`. Treat these files as the editable source of truth. Keep instructions focused and independent of any particular harness, editor or provider. Avoid product-specific commands and jargon in reusable instructions, and preserve attribution and licence notices when adapting someone else's work.

When experimenting with a skill, keep evaluation prompts and observations under `evals/`. Distinguish structural validation from observed behaviour. Add rules in response to useful preferences or demonstrated failures, and avoid building a framework before it is needed.
